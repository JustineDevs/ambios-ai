# Findings register contract

Use one row per independently verifiable issue:

| ID | Area | File/symbol | Observed | Expected | Risk | Implementation | Test | Deployment proof | Status | Blocker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Allowed status values: `observed`, `planned`, `implemented`, `verified`, `unsupported`, `blocked`.

Do not merge findings merely because they share a page. Do merge duplicate symptoms when they have one canonical root cause and one proof obligation. A “verified” status requires fresh evidence from the target environment when the claim is environment-dependent.
