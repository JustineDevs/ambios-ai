# AmbiOS AI Code of Conduct

## Purpose

AmbiOS AI is an operations product where human approvals govern agent actions against connected systems. Contributors are expected to make technical decisions, reports, and reviews safe, specific, and respectful.

## Expected conduct

- Treat contributors, users, operators, and affected third parties with respect.
- Give concrete, reproducible feedback about routes, packages, deployments, security boundaries, and behavior.
- Distinguish a defect, design disagreement, security report, and personal criticism.
- Include environment, route or Worker, reproduction steps, expected behavior, and observed behavior when reporting a problem.
- Protect credentials, OAuth tokens, customer data, incident details, and provider responses; redact them from issues, logs, screenshots, and traces.
- Accept good-faith corrections and keep discussions focused on improving the product.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, doxxing, sexualized abuse, personal attacks, deliberate exposure of private or secret data, malicious disruption, and attempts to bypass authorization or approval controls are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report security issues privately using `SECURITY.md`. Report conduct concerns privately through [@JustineDevs](https://github.com/JustineDevs) rather than posting confidential details publicly. Reports will be handled discreetly, fairly, and proportionately to the behavior and its impact. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is not acceptable.
